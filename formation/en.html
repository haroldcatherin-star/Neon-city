§ 02 — The method

Every claim gets a label.

Throughout the program, each recommendation is graded on what the research actually says. You know what's solid, what's still unproven, and what to stay away from. Click a level to filter.

Myth vs reality · 10 claims

Sunscreen is only for summer.

UV exposure is the leading driver of visible skin aging (photoaging), year-round. Daily SPF is the foundation of any routine.

ConsensusModule 02 →

Mewing reshapes an adult jaw.

No solid study shows this in adults. Resting tongue posture is a good habit, but there's no promise of skeletal remodeling.

PreliminaryModule 03 →

Bone smashing builds your cheekbones.

Zero scientific basis. Striking your own bones risks fractures, hematomas and nerve damage. Off the table, period.

WarningModule 08 →

Your jawline is pure genetics.

Bone structure won't change, but a lower body-fat percentage makes facial contours far more visible. It's often the #1 lever.

ConsensusModule 04 →

Minoxidil grows a beard.

A few small studies point that way, but it's off-label use with possible side effects. Talk to a doctor first.

PreliminaryMedical adviceModule 05 →

Chewing hard gum builds your jaw.

Masseter hypertrophy is poorly documented, while the risk of TMJ pain is real. The upside isn't worth it.

WarningModule 08 →

Bad sleep doesn't show.

Studies show sleep-deprived faces are rated as more tired and less attractive. Sleep is a simple, free lever.

PreliminaryModule 03 →

Retinoids are for people in their forties.

One of the best-studied actives for acne and texture: it speeds up keratinocyte turnover. Introduce gradually. Tretinoin requires a prescription.

ConsensusMedical adviceModule 02 →

An 800-calorie diet speeds up results.

Extreme restriction means muscle loss, fatigue, dull skin and a risk of disordered eating. A moderate deficit wins over 90 days.

WarningModule 08 →

Hair loss? Nothing you can do.

Effective DHT-related treatments exist for many men, but only under medical supervision. The earlier you see a doctor, the more options you have.

§ 08 — Questions

What people ask us.

I'm a total beginner. Is this for me?

Yes, that's who it's built for. Module 01 lays the groundwork and the 90-day plan tells you what to do each week. No equipment or prior knowledge needed.

How much will products cost?

The recommended skincare routine fits a budget of roughly $35–55 a month. For everything else (sleep, posture, training, style), we prioritize what's free or close to it.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We give you a method and a plan built on the best-documented evidence. Results depend on your consistency and your starting point.

Is this medical advice?

No. The program is aesthetic and educational. For anything health-related (severe acne, hair loss, dental issues, treatments), we refer you to a doctor, dermatologist or dentist.

What about mewing, "PSL", ratings out of 10?

We cover them, no taboo. Mewing is graded with its limitations, and we explain why constantly rating yourself does more harm than good. The goal is to improve, not to fit into a box.

I'm under 18. Can I buy it?

Yes, with a parent's consent; they make the payment. The content avoids any risky practice and stresses professional follow-up.

What format is it?

A 67-page PDF with bookmarks, so you can jump to any module. It reads well on a phone, a tablet or printed out.

Can I share it with a friend?

Your copy is licensed to you personally and carries your email on every page, so please don't. If a friend wants it, the Core tier is $29.
